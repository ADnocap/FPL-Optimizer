# Evidence for reports/2026-27_gw1-5_review.md

Verbatim outputs of the 2026-09-24/25 analysis, experiment and audit agents. Paths
inside them (SP = a session scratchpad, EXP = SP/exp) point at working files that were
not kept; the numbers and conclusions are reproduced here, and every material claim was
re-derived by an independent verifier before being used.

- `analysis_verified_full.md` — GW1-5 performance + soundness audit (7 investigators,
  adversarial + impact verification).
- `experiments/*.md` — predictor experiments on the leak-fixed harness (per-GW
  Spearman primary; paired per-GW deltas with bootstrap CIs; fast screening, full-mode
  confirmation with a different seed).
- `rules_chips_transfers_audit.md` — rules/chips audit, transfer-policy replays
  (252 season replays), chip tool, horizon planner backtests.
- `*.csv` — raw backtest tables.
